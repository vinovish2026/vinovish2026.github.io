StatVeriyan: Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 (applies from the first release of the app)

App: StatVeriyan (Android)

Publisher: VinoVish, the developer shown on the app's Google Play page

Contact for privacy questions: vinovish2026@gmail.com

The short version

StatVeriyan works offline and has no accounts. Your name, scores and settings are kept on your phone and are not sent to us; if you have Android backup switched on, Android may copy the app's saved data to your Google account. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on menu screens, a short full-screen ad now and then after a Practice round, and optional ads you choose to watch for a Practice helper. Ads use the internet. They are non-personalised, but Google may still use your advertising ID and collect device and ad interaction data to deliver and measure them; where the law asks, Google asks for your consent first. Optional purchases, such as Remove ads, are handled by Google Play, so we never see your payment details. Reminders are set on your phone. The app is for ages 13 and over.

StatVeriyan is a set of eight cricket statistics games and a reading tool called Match Notes. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we run no servers: we do not receive your name, scores or settings, and we do not sell personal information.

Ads need the internet. There are no ads in a Daily. Google, not us, handles the data that ads use (see "Ads" below). Purchases, including Remove ads, are handled by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. If you are offline, the games work exactly the same and no ad is shown.

What the app stores on your phone

To remember how you are doing, the app saves a small amount of information only on your own phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your best scores, streaks and the history of the Daily challenges you have played;
  • your profile (a display name you choose, your avatar choice and your favourite team), if you set one: this stays on the phone and is never sent to us;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, reminders, text size and similar choices);
  • what you have bought, so the app can remember it without asking Google Play every time;
  • small markers such as "has seen the how-to-play screen", how many Practice rounds you have finished and when an ad was last shown (so ads stay rare), whether you have ever chosen to watch an ad, and an unfinished game, so you can carry on after leaving the app.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We do not ask for your phone number, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. We keep it only as long as it is on your phone: until you reset it, clear the app's storage or uninstall. If you have Android backup switched on, Android may also keep a copy of it in your Google account (see "Backups" below).

Security: the saved data lives in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot normally read. Ads and purchases use encrypted connections. We run no server, so we hold no copy of your data that could be breached.

Deleting it: use Settings, Preferences, Reset stats inside the app (it erases scores, streaks, history, Daily results and unfinished games; your name, avatar and preferences are kept and are removed only by clearing storage or uninstalling), or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Purchases are kept by Google Play, not by the app: after a reset or reinstall use Restore purchases in the Store section. Step by step: the page "Delete your data" on this site.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (Google LLC). The ads are:

  • a small banner at the bottom of the Play and My Stats screens and of each game's menu (never on a screen where you are playing, on a result screen, in the Store, in Settings or in How to Play);
  • a short full-screen ad that may appear when you leave the result of a Practice round: never before your third Practice round, never within four minutes of the last one, at most one every three Practice rounds and six a day, and never within 90 seconds of an ad you chose to watch;
  • optional ads you choose to watch for a Practice helper, at most one every three minutes.

There are no ads in a Daily and none while you are playing a round. No full-screen ad appears when you open the app; a small banner sits at the bottom of the Play and My Stats screens and of each game's menu, as described above. Remove ads, or the Plus bundle, switches all of them off for good.

What Google may collect when an ad is requested or shown: your phone's advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which can show roughly where you are, for example your city), device and app information (model, operating system version, app name and version), how you interact with an ad (for example that it was shown or tapped), and diagnostic information such as app launch time and crashes of the ad software, so that the ad can be delivered, measured, kept from being shown too often and protected against fraud. This data goes to Google; we do not receive it. Google handles it under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The details of what the SDK declares are on AdMob's page "Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Play" (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). Google may process this data in countries other than yours. The ad software sends it over an encrypted connection.

The ads are non-personalised. The app asks Google for ads that are not chosen from your past behaviour. Google may still use data such as the advertising ID for the purposes above (limiting how often an ad is shown, measuring ads, preventing fraud).

Consent. Where the law asks for it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice later under Settings, Privacy and ad choices, which appears when the consent platform says it is required. On your phone you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

Age. The app is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and ask for content suitable for general audiences at most.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (the thank-yous, Remove ads, the Plus bundle and the cosmetic packs) are bought through the Google Play purchase screen and are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card or other payment details. The app asks the Google Play app on your phone which purchases your Google account has made for this app, so that they can be restored on a new phone. We do not run a server and keep no list of buyers. The price is shown in your local currency as set in Google Play. A thank-you adds a supporter star to your profile on your phone, Remove ads switches ads off, and the cosmetic packs change how the app looks. Nothing you buy changes a score or a Daily, and nothing gives an advantage in any game. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own rules.

Reminders (notifications)

If you switch on a reminder in Settings, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and then schedules the reminder on your phone. Nothing is sent over the internet for this, and no push-notification service is used. Reminders are off until you switch one on. Turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings at any time. The app also lets Android restart a scheduled reminder after the phone restarts.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no user profiles visible to others and no user-generated content.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting software of its own, and it does not build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites. (The ad SDK is described above.)
  • It does not read your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, calendar or location.
  • It does not sell personal information.

Sharing a result (only if you choose to)

Each game has a Share result button. When you press it, the app gives a short piece of plain text (for example the game name, your score and a link to the app's page in Google Play) to the Android share sheet, and you choose which app, if any, receives it. If you pick another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send through it. If sharing is not possible the app copies the text to your clipboard. The app never shares anything unless you press the button.

Backups

This app leaves Android's automatic backup at its default, which is on. So, if you have turned backup on in your Android settings, Android can copy the app's saved data (your name, scores, settings and what you have bought, as listed above) to your Google account, and restore it to a new phone. That is a feature of Android and of your Google account, controlled by you; the publisher of this app does not receive that data and cannot see it. To remove such a copy, use your Google account's backup settings (see the page "Delete your data").

Where the cricket data comes from

The statistics in the games are bundled inside the app and are derived from ball-by-ball records published by Cricsheet (https://cricsheet.org) under the Open Data Commons Attribution License, with player names from Wikidata (CC0). The full credits and licences are on the About and Data sources and licences screens in the app. StatVeriyan is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any cricket league, board, team or player. The data describes public sporting events; the app contains no personal data about its users.

Children

StatVeriyan is a general-audience cricket statistics game for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts and no chat, so a child cannot be contacted through it, and the ads it shows are non-personalised and limited to content suitable for general audiences. We do not track or profile anyone. If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. If you are under the age at which you may make purchases in your country, please ask a parent or guardian before buying anything; you can always keep playing without it.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you on our side: the game information is on your phone and you control it as described above. For data that Google processes when an ad is shown or you buy something, use the controls in your Google account (https://myaccount.google.com), the ad choices in Android settings and Settings, Privacy and ad choices in the app, and Google's privacy policy. Depending on where you live you may have rights to see, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data-protection authority; because we hold none, the way to exercise them for ad data is through Google. If you have a question about this policy, want to tell us about a problem or want to complain, write to vinovish2026@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example personalised advertising, analytics, accounts, online play or a server), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date at the top will change.
