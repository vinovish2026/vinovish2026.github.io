CricVeriyan: Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (applies from the first release of the app with accounts)

App: CricVeriyan (Android)

Publisher: VinoVish, the developer shown on the app's Google Play page

Contact for privacy questions: vinovish2026@gmail.com

The short version

CricVeriyan asks you to sign in with Google once and pick a nickname. After that the games work offline. Your progress is saved to your account on Google Firebase, with the database in Mumbai, so a new phone can get it back and you can play with friends. Other players see only your nickname and the scores you play with them: in challenges, in tournaments, and your weekly Daily total if you share it with friends. The app does not read your Google name, e-mail or photo, but Firebase keeps the sign-in details Google passes on. Scores are what each phone reports and are not checked. You can delete your account and its online copy in the app, under Settings, Account, or by e-mail. If you have Android backup switched on, Android may also copy the app's saved data to your Google account. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on menu screens, a short full-screen ad now and then after a Practice round, and optional ads you choose to watch: for a Practice helper, to see the best answer after a Practice round, for a bonus on a Daily result, or for a Rain delay. Ads use the internet. They are non-personalised, but Google may still use your advertising ID and collect device and ad interaction data to deliver and measure them; where the law asks, Google asks for your consent first. Optional purchases, such as Remove ads, are handled by Google Play, so we never see your payment details. Reminders are set on your phone. The app is for ages 18 and over.

CricVeriyan is a set of eight cricket statistics games and a reading tool called Match Notes. You need a Google account to use it. The developer receives and stores the account data described below, we do not sell personal information, and there is no chat: players cannot send each other messages.

Signing in, saving your progress, friends, challenges and tournaments need the internet. Playing the games does not, once you have signed in. There are no ads in a Daily. Google, not us, handles the data that ads use (see "Ads" below). Purchases, including Remove ads, are handled by Google Play, and we never see your payment details.

Who the app is for

CricVeriyan is for people aged 18 and over. When you sign in, you confirm on the sign-in screen that you are 18 or older and accept the Terms of use. We do not check your age. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may delete it. See "Children" below.

Your account and what we keep online

When you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). We then keep the following about you in our Cloud Firestore database on Google Firebase, with the database in Mumbai, India (asia-south1):

  • Your sign-in. Firebase Authentication keeps a record for your account: a user ID, the e-mail address, name and profile-photo link that Google passes on, the sign-in provider and the times you signed up and last signed in. Firebase also processes your IP address for security and to prevent abuse. The app never reads your Google name, e-mail address or photo, never shows them to anyone and does not copy them into our database. The developer can see the Firebase sign-in record in the Firebase console.
  • Your nickname, which you choose and which must be unique, and the time you set or last changed it.
  • Your friends, the friend requests you send and receive, and the players you block, each by user ID and nickname.
  • Your challenges: for each challenge you send or receive, who it is between, the game, the round (a seed and a data version), the scores of both players, any reaction either player gave (one of eight fixed answers), its status and its dates.
  • Your tournaments: for each tournament you host or are invited to, its name (picked from a fixed list), its format and rounds (each a game, a seed and a data version), who hosted it, its members by user ID and nickname, whether you joined or left, your score in each round, the reactions you gave (one of eight fixed answers per score) and the dates. Nothing in a tournament is typed by a player.
  • Your weekly totals, while "Share my weekly scores" is On, which is the default: for the current week, the score of each of that day's three Featured Dailies (the game and the score) and their sum. It is one record that is replaced as the weeks go on.
  • Your cloud save: the progress that is also kept on your phone, such as your Runs, career rank and XP, the short list of what they were earned or spent on, your streaks, your Campaign progress, your best scores and Daily results, your profile (the display name you may set, your avatar and your favourite team), your settings, the looks you have earned and picked, small markers such as "has seen the how-to-play screen" and the two ad-consent markers (whether you chose to see personalised ads, and whether your region needs the ad privacy choice), and how many thank-you tips you have bought. Weekly copies of the previous state of your save are kept so that a bad sync can be undone. Your record of wins, losses and draws against each friend is not part of it: it stays on your phone.
  • Reports you send, with the short text you wrote (up to 300 characters), who you reported and why.
  • Counters and a ban list. Daily counters of how many requests, challenges and tournaments you started (to stop spam), with the internal ID of the last player, challenge or tournament, and, for an account we have banned, its user ID.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. Your purchases are held by Google Play, not by us, with one exception: how many thank-you tips you have bought is part of your cloud save, so the supporter mark comes back on a new phone.

Why we keep it: to sign you in, to give you your nickname, to save and restore your progress, to let you play with friends, in challenges, tournaments and the weekly friends board, and to keep the app safe (reports, blocks and abuse prevention). We do not use it for advertising and we do not build a profile of you.

Security: the connection between the app and Firebase is encrypted. Access rules on the database let you read and change your own save and let other players see only what is described under "Who can see what". Google encrypts stored data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that data will never be lost or exposed.

Who can see what

  • Other players see only your nickname to know who you are: the people you send a friend request to, your friends, and the other members of a tournament you are in. Anyone who types your exact nickname can find out that the nickname exists and which internal account ID owns it, but nothing else about you. Your friends' phones also receive the dates your profile was created and your nickname last changed; the app does not show them.
  • The two players in a challenge see each other's score in that challenge, and the reaction either of them gave. Nobody else sees them.
  • The members of a tournament see each other's nickname, the score of each round and the reactions, inside that tournament and nowhere else. Every member is a friend of the host, but members do not have to be friends with each other, so a tournament can show your nickname and your scores to players who are not your friends. They see nothing else about you: no profile, totals or e-mail address. The host sees what the other members see, and can invite friends and cancel the tournament, but cannot change a score or a round. You can leave a tournament at any time. A player you have blocked cannot invite you to one.
  • Your friends can see your weekly totals while "Share my weekly scores" is On. The app shows a friend only your nickname, your weekly total and how many days you played; the stored record also holds the day-by-day scores of the three Featured Dailies, which a friend's phone can read although the app does not show them. In the app, a friend's scores for today stay hidden until you have finished your own three Dailies. A friend you have blocked cannot read it. Switching the setting Off deletes the stored record.
  • Reactions are one of eight fixed answers, and tournament names come from a fixed list: nothing is typed, so there are no messages between players. Your win, loss and draw record against each friend stays on your phone and nobody else sees it.
  • Scores in challenges, tournaments and on the friends board are what each phone reports. They are not checked, so a trophy or a place on a board means "among friends", not "proved".
  • Nobody sees your Google name, e-mail address or photo through the app, and no one sees your cloud save, your Runs, rank, Campaign progress or other scores.
  • The developer, who runs the Firebase project, can open the database and the sign-in record in the Firebase console, for example to answer a report or a deletion request. Google, as our service provider, processes the data for us under Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Your sign-in record, nickname, friends, friend requests you sent, blocked players, challenges and cloud save are kept until you delete your account, and are then deleted (with the exceptions below).
  • Friend requests that nobody answers run out after about 30 days.
  • A challenge runs out about 14 days after it was sent and is then deleted.
  • A tournament, with its members' records (scores and reactions), is deleted about 60 days after it was made. The app hides finished tournaments after 30 days.
  • Tournament tables you played in keep your nickname and scores until the tournament is removed automatically, after about 60 days. A finished tournament you hosted stays for the others in the same way; one still running is cancelled and removed.
  • Your weekly totals stay until you switch "Share my weekly scores" Off or delete your account, and a new week replaces the old one.
  • The weekly copies of your cloud save are deleted after about 90 days.
  • A report, whether you sent it or someone sent it about you, is kept for about 180 days, even if the account it came from or is about has been deleted, so that we can keep the app safe and stop abuse. This is the main exception to deletion.
  • Today's counter of the requests, challenges and tournaments you started cannot be deleted on the same day (it stops the daily limit being reset); it is removed automatically within a few days.
  • If we banned an account, the user ID on a ban list stays after the account is deleted, as a record for handling abuse. It names no person.
  • A friend's copy of your friend link, and a request you sent, can remain until it expires.
  • Something on another player's side that points to you, such as their own note that they once sent you a friend request, a friend link left over from an unfriend that did not finish, or a reaction they gave to a score of yours in a tournament (it sits in their own record and names no one in the app), can stay in their account until they remove it or the tournament runs out.
  • Google may keep copies of deleted data in its own backups for a limited time under its own terms.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. The page tells you what goes. You type your nickname, Google asks you to sign in once more, and then your nickname, friends (on both sides), the friend requests you sent and received, your blocked list, your challenges (sent and received), your tournaments (a running one you host is cancelled and deleted; from the others you leave; the tables you played in, and a finished tournament you hosted, keep your nickname and scores until the tournament is removed automatically, see "How long we keep it"), your shared weekly totals and your cloud save with its weekly copies are deleted, and finally your sign-in record. Your friends no longer see you.
  • By e-mail: if you cannot open the app, write to vinovish2026@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your Google account, with the subject "Delete my CricVeriyan account" and your nickname. We delete the same things by hand and aim to do it within 30 days. The step-by-step page is "Delete your account" on this site.
  • What stays: the progress on your phone stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or use Reset all; the exceptions under "How long we keep it" (reports, tournament tables you played in, a finished tournament you hosted, today's counter, the ban list, records on other players' side, Google's backups); anything you shared with another app; purchases, which Google Play holds; and the data ads use, which Google holds.

What the app stores on your phone

To remember how you are doing, the app saves a small amount of information on your own phone, in the app's private storage, and, once you are signed in, also in your cloud save as described above:

  • your best scores, streaks and the history of the Daily challenges you have played;
  • your Runs, career rank and XP, a short list of what they were earned or spent on, and the looks you have earned;
  • your profile (a display name you choose, your avatar choice and your favourite team), if you set one: other players do not see it, only your nickname;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, reminders, text size and similar choices);
  • your win, loss and draw record against each friend, and the last friends board and tournaments you opened, kept so those screens open quickly (removed when you sign out or delete your account);
  • what you have bought, so the app can remember it without asking Google Play every time (this is not copied to your cloud save, except how many thank-yous you have given);
  • small markers such as "has seen the how-to-play screen", how many Practice rounds you have finished and when an ad was last shown (so ads stay rare), whether you have ever chosen to watch an ad, and an unfinished game, so you can carry on after leaving the app.

Security: the saved data lives in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot normally read.

Deleting it: use Settings, Preferences, Reset stats inside the app (it erases scores, streaks, history, Daily results and unfinished games, on your phone and in your cloud save; your name, avatar, preferences and Campaign progress are kept), or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete your account or its online copy: for that, delete your account as described above. Purchases are kept by Google Play, not by the app: after a reset or reinstall use Restore purchases in the Store section. Step by step: the page "Delete your account" on this site.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (Google LLC). The ads are:

  • a small banner at the bottom of the Play and My Stats screens and of each game's menu (never on a screen where you are playing, on a result screen, in the Store, in Settings or in How to Play);
  • a short full-screen ad that may appear when you leave the result of a Practice round: never before your third Practice round, never within four minutes of the last one, at most one every three Practice rounds and six a day, and never within 90 seconds of an ad you chose to watch;
  • optional ads you choose to watch, at most one every three minutes in all: for a helper in a Practice round (an extra wicket, clue, Joker, miss or scout report), offered at the moment it would help, never in a challenge or Campaign round, and played only if you tap it; to see the best answer after a Practice round (the result of a Practice round may offer "Watch an ad" as the alternative to spending Runs on that reveal; never after a Daily, a challenge or a Campaign round, and played only if you tap it); for a bonus after a Daily (the result of a Daily shows a "Claim bonus" button with a small ad symbol, and the ad plays only if you tap that button); or for a Rain delay, which keeps your streak (on the Rain delay screen, again only if you tap it).

There are no ads in a Daily and none that appear on their own on a Daily's result screen. No ad ever appears on its own while you answer or pick: the only ad connected to a Daily is the optional bonus ad above, which plays only if you tap "Claim bonus" on the result. No full-screen ad appears when you open the app; a small banner sits at the bottom of the Play and My Stats screens and of each game's menu, as described above. Remove ads, or the Plus bundle, switches all of them off for good.

What Google may collect when an ad is requested or shown: your phone's advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which can show roughly where you are, for example your city), device and app information (model, operating system version, app name and version), how you interact with an ad (for example that it was shown or tapped), and diagnostic information such as app launch time and crashes of the ad software, so that the ad can be delivered, measured, kept from being shown too often and protected against fraud. This data goes to Google; we do not receive it. Google handles it under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The details of what the SDK declares are on AdMob's page "Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Play" (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). Google may process this data in countries other than yours. The ad software sends it over an encrypted connection. We do not give the ad software your nickname or your saved progress.

The ads are non-personalised. The app asks Google for ads that are not chosen from your past behaviour. Google may still use data such as the advertising ID for the purposes above (limiting how often an ad is shown, measuring ads, preventing fraud).

Consent. Where the law asks for it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice later under Settings, Privacy and ad choices, which appears when the consent platform says it is required. On your phone you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

Age. The app is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and ask for content suitable for general audiences at most.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (the thank-yous, Remove ads, the Plus bundle and the cosmetic packs) are bought through the Google Play purchase screen and are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card or other payment details. The app asks the Google Play app on your phone which purchases your Google account has made for this app, so that they can be restored on a new phone. We keep no list of buyers. The price is shown in your local currency as set in Google Play. A thank-you adds a supporter star to your profile on your phone, Remove ads switches ads off, and the cosmetic packs change how the app looks. Nothing you buy changes a score or a Daily, and nothing gives an advantage in any game. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own rules.

Reminders (notifications)

If you switch on a reminder in Settings, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and then schedules the reminder on your phone. Nothing is sent over the internet for this, and no push-notification service is used. Reminders are off until you switch one on. Turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings at any time. The app also lets Android restart a scheduled reminder after the phone restarts. Friend requests, challenges and tournament invitations are not announced by notification: you see them when you open the app.

What the app does not do

  • It has no chat, no messages between players and no profile pictures or free text that other players can see: the only thing that names you to others is your nickname, next to the scores you play with them.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting software of its own, and it does not build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites. (The ad SDK and Firebase are described above.)
  • It does not read your Google name, e-mail address, photo, phone number, contacts, files, microphone, camera, calendar or location.
  • It does not sell personal information.

Sharing a result (only if you choose to)

Each game has a Share result button. When you press it, the app gives a short piece of plain text (for example the game name, your score and a link to the app's page in Google Play) to the Android share sheet, and you choose which app, if any, receives it. If you pick another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send through it. If sharing is not possible the app copies the text to your clipboard. The app never shares anything unless you press the button.

Backups

This app leaves Android's automatic backup at its default, which is on. So, if you have turned backup on in your Android settings, Android can copy the app's saved data (your name, scores, settings and what you have bought, as listed above) to your Google account, and restore it to a new phone. That is a feature of Android and of your Google account, controlled by you; the publisher of this app does not receive that data and cannot see it. It is separate from your CricVeriyan account and is not removed when you delete that account. To remove such a copy, use your Google account's backup settings (see the page "Delete your account").

Where the cricket data comes from

The statistics in the games are bundled inside the app and are derived from ball-by-ball records published by Cricsheet (https://cricsheet.org) under the Open Data Commons Attribution License, with player names from Wikidata (CC0). The full credits and licences are on the About and Data sources and licences screens in the app. CricVeriyan is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any cricket league, board, team or player. The data describes public sporting events; it is not data about the app's users.

Children

CricVeriyan is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and the account sign-in asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not check ages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has an account, write to vinovish2026@gmail.com and we will delete it. The ads the app shows are non-personalised and limited to content suitable for general audiences. We do not track or profile anyone.

Your rights

You can see and change your nickname in the app, delete your account in the app, and reset your stats in the app. You can also write to vinovish2026@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to ask us to correct it or to delete it, and we aim to reply within 30 days. For data that Google processes when an ad is shown or you buy something, use the controls in your Google account (https://myaccount.google.com), the ad choices in Android settings and Settings, Privacy and ad choices in the app, and Google's privacy policy. Depending on where you live you may have rights to see, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data-protection authority. If you have a question about this policy, want to tell us about a problem or want to complain, write to vinovish2026@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
