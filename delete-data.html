Delete your data

StatVeriyan has no accounts and no servers of its own, so there is no online account to close and nothing stored about you on our side. Everything the app keeps is on your phone (plus any copy Android's own backup made, see section 5), and you can erase it yourself.

1. Erase your scores and history (the app stays)

  1. Open StatVeriyan and go to Settings, then Preferences.
  2. Choose Reset stats and confirm.

This erases your best scores, streaks, history, Daily results and unfinished games. Your name, avatar and preferences are kept.

2. Erase everything the app keeps on the phone

Either of these removes your name, avatar, preferences, scores and every other marker the app saved:

  • Clear storage: in Android, open Settings, Apps, StatVeriyan, Storage, then Clear storage (the wording differs a little between phones).
  • Uninstall the app.

3. Purchases

Purchases are recorded by Google Play, not by the app, and the app never sees your payment details. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete a purchase from your Google account; use Settings, Store, Restore purchases in the app to get it back on the same Google account. Questions about a payment or a refund go to Google Play.

4. Ads

The ads come from Google AdMob. We do not receive the data they use and cannot delete it. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android (Settings, Google, Ads), change your consent under Settings, Privacy and ad choices in the app where it appears, and manage your data in your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com. Buying Remove ads stops the app requesting ads.

5. Backups

If you have switched on Android backup to your Google account, a copy of the app's saved data may exist there. Remove it in your Google account's backup settings (Google Drive, Backups).
