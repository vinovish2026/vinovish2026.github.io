Delete your account

This page is for the Android app CricVeriyan, published by <DEVELOPER NAME> (the developer shown on its Google Play page). It explains how to delete your CricVeriyan account and the data saved with it, from inside the app or by e-mail, and what is deleted and what is kept.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open CricVeriyan and go to Settings, then Account.
  2. Choose Delete account. The page shows exactly what will be deleted.
  3. Type your nickname to confirm, then sign in with Google once more when it asks (this proves it is you). You need the internet for this.

If it stops halfway (for example you lose the connection), the app offers Finish deleting the next time you open it, and nothing is written back to your account in the meantime.

2. Delete your account by e-mail

If you cannot open the app, or you have already uninstalled it, write to <OWNER E-MAIL> from the e-mail address of the Google account you used to sign in. Use the subject Delete my CricVeriyan account and put your nickname in the message. We delete the account by hand and aim to do it within 30 days, and we will write back to confirm. You do not need to reinstall the app.

3. What is deleted

  • Your nickname, which becomes free for anyone to use.
  • Your friends (on both sides of each friendship), the friend requests you sent and received, and the players you blocked. Your friends no longer see you.
  • Your challenges, sent and received (a challenge also runs out by itself about 14 days after it was sent).
  • Your tournaments: a running one you host is cancelled and deleted, and from the others you leave (what stays of those tables, and of a finished tournament you hosted, is listed in section 4; a tournament also runs out by itself about 60 days after it was made).
  • Your shared weekly totals for the friends board (you can also delete them at any time by switching off Share my weekly scores).
  • Your cloud save: Runs, rank, Campaign progress, scores, Daily results, profile, settings and the weekly copies of it.
  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication (your user ID and the details Google passed on).

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • A report that you sent about another player, or that someone sent about you, is kept for about 180 days so that we can keep the app safe and stop abuse.
  • Today's counter of the requests, challenges and tournaments you started cannot be deleted on the same day (it stops the daily limit being reset). It holds internal account IDs only, including the last player you sent something to, and is removed automatically within a few days.
  • If your account was banned, its user ID stays on the ban list as a record for handling abuse. It names no person.
  • Tournament tables you played in keep your nickname and scores until the tournament is removed automatically, after about 60 days. A finished tournament you hosted stays for the others in the same way; one still running is cancelled and removed.
  • A friend's copy of your friend link, and a request you sent, can remain until it expires.
  • Something on another player's side that points to you, such as their own note that they once sent you a friend request, a friend link left over from an unfriend that did not finish, or a reaction they gave to a score of yours in a tournament (it sits in their own record and names no one in the app), can stay in their account until they remove it or the tournament runs out.
  • Google may keep copies of deleted data in its own backups for a limited time under its own terms.

5. Data on your phone

Deleting your account does not remove what is saved on your phone, so the progress you made stays with you. To erase that too, use one of these:

  • Reset stats: in the app go to Settings, then Preferences, and choose Reset stats. It erases scores, streaks, history, Daily results and unfinished games (your name, avatar, preferences and Campaign progress are kept).
  • Clear storage: in Android, open Settings, Apps, CricVeriyan, Storage, then Clear storage (the wording differs a little between phones).
  • Uninstall the app. This also removes your win, loss and draw record against each friend, which is kept on the phone only.

6. Purchases

Purchases are recorded by Google Play, not by the app or your CricVeriyan account, and the app never sees your payment details. Deleting your account does not delete a purchase from your Google account; use Settings, Store, Restore purchases in the app to get it back on the same Google account. Questions about a payment or a refund go to Google Play.

7. Ads

The ads come from Google AdMob. We do not receive the data they use and cannot delete it. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android (Settings, Google, Ads), change your consent under Settings, Privacy and ad choices in the app where it appears, and manage your data in your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com. Buying Remove ads stops the app requesting ads.

8. Android backups

If you have switched on Android backup to your Google account, a copy of the app's saved data may exist there. It is separate from your CricVeriyan account. Remove it in your Google account's backup settings (Google Drive, Backups).
