CricVeriyan

CricVeriyan is a set of eight cricket statistics games for Android, with a daily challenge and a reading tool called Match Notes. You sign in with Google once and pick a nickname; after that the games work offline. The statistics are counted from public ball-by-ball records. It is for people aged 18 and over.

The app and your privacy

  • You sign in with Google once and pick a unique nickname. Your progress is saved to your account on Google Firebase (database in Mumbai) so a new phone can get it back. Other players see only your nickname and the scores you play with them (challenges, tournaments and a weekly friends board), and there is no chat.
  • The app contains ads from Google AdMob (non-personalised, none in a Daily) and optional in-app purchases through Google Play, such as Remove ads.
  • Read the privacy policy for exactly what is stored, where, for how long, what the ads involve and what Google may collect, and the terms of use for the rules.
  • See Delete your account for how to delete your account, in the app or by e-mail, and everything else the app keeps.

About the data

Match data contains information from Cricsheet (https://cricsheet.org), made available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY 1.0). Player names come from Wikidata (CC0). The full credits are in the app, under About.

CricVeriyan is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any cricket league, board, team or player. Team and player names are used only to identify players and teams in the statistics, and belong to their owners. It is for fun and trivia only: no real-money play, betting or prizes.
