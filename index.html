StatVeriyan

StatVeriyan is a set of eight cricket statistics games for Android that work offline, with a daily challenge and a reading tool called Match Notes. The statistics are counted from public ball-by-ball records.

The app and your privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your scores, streaks and settings are kept on your phone and are not sent to us (Android's own backup may copy them to your Google account if you have backup switched on). The app is for ages 13 and over.
  • The app contains ads from Google AdMob (non-personalised, none in a Daily) and optional in-app purchases through Google Play, such as Remove ads.
  • Read the privacy policy for exactly what is stored, what the ads involve and what Google may collect.
  • See Delete your data for how to erase everything the app keeps.

About the data

Match data contains information from Cricsheet (https://cricsheet.org), made available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY 1.0). Player names come from Wikidata (CC0). The full credits are in the app, under About.

StatVeriyan is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any cricket league, board, team or player. Team and player names are used only to identify players and teams in the statistics, and belong to their owners. It is for fun and trivia only: no real-money play, betting or prizes.
