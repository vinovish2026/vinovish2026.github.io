CricVeriyan: Terms of use

These are the rules for using CricVeriyan, a set of eight cricket statistics games for Android. They are the same words as the Terms of use page inside the app. The privacy policy explains how your data is handled.

Who can use CricVeriyan

CricVeriyan is for people aged 18 and over. When you sign in, you confirm on the sign-in screen that you are 18 or older and accept these terms. We do not check your age, so please be honest.

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. We keep your nickname and your saved progress under your account so you can get them back on a new phone. You can delete your account in Settings, Account, Delete account, or by writing to the contact address below. How we handle your data is described in the privacy policy.

Your nickname

Your nickname is the only name other players see. Pick a name that is yours. Please do not use a real person's name, a team, a company or a brand, and nothing rude or hateful.

Other players

You can send a friend request to a player you know by nickname, and challenges and tournament invitations to your friends. Players in a tournament see each other's nickname, scores and reactions there, even if they are not friends with each other. Reactions are chosen from a fixed list. Players cannot send each other messages. You can block a player, and report a friend, a tournament host or a player who sent you a request or a challenge. We may remove a nickname, or stop an account, that breaks these rules.

Scores

A challenge, tournament or friends board score is what each player's phone reports. It is not checked. They are for fun and only earn a few Runs. If you share your weekly scores, your friends can see them; you can switch that off at any time.

No promises

CricVeriyan is provided as it is. We try to keep your saved progress safe, but we cannot promise the app will always work or that data will never be lost. We may pause or change online features, such as cloud saving, friends, challenges and tournaments, for maintenance or when a service limit is reached; the games keep working offline. We may change or stop features.
